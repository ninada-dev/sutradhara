Brew Guides

Mastering the art of brewing is a rewarding journey. Whether you prefer the clarity of a pour-over, the richness of a French press, or the intensity of an espresso, our guides will help you get the most out of your beans. Each method requires a different approach, from grind size to water temperature, and we are here to guide you through every step. We believe that everyone can brew a great cup of coffee at home with the right knowledge and tools. That's why we've put together these comprehensive guides, designed to empower you to experiment and find your perfect brew. Explore our techniques, learn the science behind each method, and elevate your daily coffee ritual. We are constantly updating our guides with new tips and tricks, so be sure to check back often. If you have any questions or need personalized advice, don't hesitate to reach out to our team of experts. We are always happy to help you on your coffee journey. Remember, the best cup of coffee is the one you enjoy the most, so don't be afraid to experiment and find what works for you. Happy brewing!

Pour over is a delicate method that highlights the subtle notes of our single-origin beans. Use a medium-fine grind and hot water (around 92°C). Pour slowly in circular motions to ensure even extraction. The result is a clean, bright cup with complex flavors.

French press is full-bodied and robust, perfect for those who love a rich, textured cup. Use a coarse grind and steep for 4 minutes. Press gently and serve immediately. This method preserves the natural oils of the coffee, resulting in a deep, flavorful experience.

Espresso is concentrated and intense, the foundation of many classic coffee drinks. Use a fine grind and high pressure to extract the essence of the coffee. The result is a bold, syrupy shot with a beautiful crema. Perfect for a quick pick-me-up or as a base for lattes and cappuccinos.

What is your roasting process? +
We roast in small batches using traditional drum roasters, ensuring optimal flavor development and consistency. Every roast is carefully monitored by our head roaster to bring out the unique characteristics of each origin.
Do you ship across India? +
Yes, we ship to all major cities in India via reliable courier partners. We take great care in packaging our coffee to ensure it reaches you fresh and in perfect condition.
